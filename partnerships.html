Partnerships

Better care technology
starts with listening.

Work with us to explore Bedbord or TESS in your hospital, care home or care organisation. A useful partnership starts with a shared understanding of the problem.

An approach, not a fixed timetable

From first conversation
to an informed decision.

01

Listen & understand

Map the people, setting and current workflow. Agree the problem worth addressing and the product that fits.

02

Scope & assess

Review infrastructure, information governance, accessibility, safety and commercial requirements with the right people.

03

Pilot & learn

Define a small pilot, training and success measures. Compare a baseline with feedback and observations during use.

04

Review & decide

Consider benefits, burden, costs and any unresolved risks. Decide together whether to refine, expand or stop.

Timelines, responsibilities and support arrangements are agreed in the proposal. We do not assume access to patient data or approval to deploy from a product enquiry.
