What authorised users can do

From onboarding
to everyday insight.

Permissions should match each person’s responsibility, with a record of important actions.

Maintain resident profiles

Record the minimum information needed to personalise TESS, such as name and date of birth, only for residents with an approved lawful basis and authorised access.

Manage devices & licences

Register each tablet, assign it to a facility, monitor licence status and keep a clear inventory of active devices.

Review service insight

See aggregate activity trends, feature use and engagement patterns to support local service improvement—not clinical decision-making.

Access is issued after onboarding

A safer way to start.

  1. Agree your facility setup

    Confirm the organisation, the authorised administrators, devices and licence terms.

  2. Invite named users

    Each administrator receives their own account. Shared usernames and passwords should not be used.

  3. Register devices & residents

    Set up TESS tablets and only the resident data needed for the agreed service.

  4. Review and improve

    Use facility-level trends to guide training, activity planning and support conversations.

Already an onboarded facility?

Your facility administrator can provide the secure portal address and help with access. If you have lost access, contact TruSelv support from your registered work email.

Request access support

Never send usernames, passwords, resident records or date-of-birth information by email.

Privacy and governance

Built for responsible use.

The facility portal must be a separate, protected application. Resident data stays within the facility boundary, access is role based, and every sensitive action is auditable.
