A little more detail

Your TESS questions.

What is included in the £119 offer?

One TESS tablet. The photos show a keyboard, mouse, stylus, case and cable; the final accessory bundle and hardware specification will be confirmed before payment.

Is TESS a medical treatment?

TESS is presented here as an engagement and communication experience. We do not claim it diagnoses, treats or prevents any condition. Activities should be chosen around individual preferences and support needs.

Can we use TESS in a care home?

TESS is designed with shared and supported use in care settings in mind. Ask us about device management, hygiene instructions, account permissions and shared-device use before ordering for your organisation.

Can I try it before buying?

Yes—request a free demonstration and tell us who will be using TESS. We can discuss the activities, interaction options and requirements relevant to your setting.

Do I need an internet connection?

Online features, downloads and streaming require a suitable internet connection. Ask us to confirm any offline activities in the current app release.
