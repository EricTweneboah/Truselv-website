Our purpose

Digital healthcare.
Person-centred only.

TruSelv develops digital experiences around the person receiving care. We want important information to feel clearer, participation to feel easier and care environments to feel more connected.

Why we exist

Technology should leave
room for humanity.

TruSelv grew from experience on hospital wards, where small communication gaps can shape someone’s day.

A name that is hard to read. A preference that gets missed. An opportunity for a conversation that never quite happens.

Our focus is practical: Bedbord for a clearer and more personal bedside view, and TESS for communication and meaningful engagement. Each gives people another way to be seen and involved.

What guides us

Useful. Considered. Human.

Start with the person

Listen to individual preferences and lived experience. Make space for choice, agency and dignity.

Make things clearer

Use plain language, a calm interface and a clear purpose. Useful technology should be understandable.

Earn confidence

Be precise about capabilities, evidence and responsibilities. Good decisions need honest information.
