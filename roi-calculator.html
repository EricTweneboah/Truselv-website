Transparent by design

What goes into the estimate.

Annual hours

Beds × occupancy ÷ 100 × operating days × (current minutes − digital minutes) ÷ 60.

First-year value

Released hours × staff cost, plus avoidable consumables, minus annual running costs and one-off project costs.

ROI and payback

First-year ROI = net modelled value ÷ total first-year costs × 100. Payback = one-off costs ÷ annual net benefit before one-off costs × 12 months. Payback is only shown when annual net benefit is positive and an upfront cost has been entered.

What this leaves out

Financing, discounting, tax, cost inflation and any unentered costs. Time released is staff capacity and only becomes a cash saving if your organisation can actually reduce expenditure. This is not a product performance study.
