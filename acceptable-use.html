Acceptable use

Practical boundaries for using the website and discussing Bedbord and TESS.

Updated 15 September 2026 Version 1.0

On this page

Respect people and information Care and professional judgement Accounts and devices Reporting a concern

Respect people and information

Do not submit another person’s medical records, health information or identifying screenshots through the marketing website. Share only the information necessary for the enquiry and only information you are entitled to share.

Do not use the website or products to harass, discriminate, exploit, impersonate or distribute unlawful content. Respect intellectual property and the privacy of residents, patients, families and staff.

Care and professional judgement

Bedbord does not replace approved records, patient identification or clinical verification. TESS activities do not replace a clinician, carer, qualified interpreter or safeguarding process.

AI-generated content can be inaccurate or unsuitable. Do not use it for diagnosis, medication, treatment or emergency decisions. Supervise use where appropriate, and stop an activity if it causes discomfort or distress.

Accounts and devices

Follow the product’s supplied instructions and your organisation’s access policies. Do not share staff credentials, bypass controls, install unapproved software or attempt to access another person’s information.

Before shared use, confirm appropriate accounts, content, permissions, cleaning and secure reset procedures. Only use approved power supplies and compatible accessories.

Reporting a concern

If a concern affects immediate care, use the local clinical or emergency escalation route first. For product issues, contact support@truselv.co.uk with a factual description that excludes patient data. Product-specific contractual enforcement and suspension provisions must be set out in the relevant service agreement.
