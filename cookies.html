Cookies & local storage

A straightforward explanation of the technology used by this website.

Updated 15 September 2026 Version 1.0

On this page

What this website uses Cookie preferences Other websites and services Your browser controls

What this website uses

The marketing website code does not set cookies, use localStorage or sessionStorage, or install analytics, advertising or social media tracking scripts. When you choose secure checkout, Stripe’s embedded payment form loads and may use cookies and similar technology necessary for payment and fraud prevention. See Stripe’s cookie policy. It does not use third-party video embeds. Its fonts, images and videos are served as site assets.

A temporary basket quantity, calculator values and form entries are held in the current page’s memory. Reloading or leaving the page clears them. Browsers may independently retain autofill details under your own browser settings.

Cookie preferences

There are no optional tracking categories to accept or reject in this version. The Cookie preferences control in the footer explains this. It does not write a consent cookie because there are no optional tracking services to activate.

If optional tracking is introduced later, this policy and any necessary controls must be updated before it runs.

Other websites and services

Opening an email application or app-store page takes you into another service. Payment checkout embeds Stripe within the shop page. That service may use its own storage, cookies and privacy controls. Your browser also caches downloaded resources to improve performance.

Hosting infrastructure may process connection information to deliver and secure the site. See the privacy notice for more detail.

Your browser controls

You can control cookies, saved form data and cache through your browser. The information and product pages remain readable without optional storage. Contact support@truselv.co.uk if you notice a service that does not match this description.

Learn more from the ICO’s explanation of cookies.
