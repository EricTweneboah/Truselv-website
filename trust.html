Trust centre

Clear information.
Considered decisions.

Trust is built through evidence, good questions and clear responsibilities. Here is how to approach the assessment of Bedbord and TESS.

A transparent starting point

Ask for evidence.
Know what it covers.

Requirements depend on the product, software release, intended use and deployment setting.

This website does not publish verified ISO certificates, NHS DSPT status, a completed DTAC assessment, product conformity declarations or a clinical safety case. Their availability and applicability must be confirmed for the product you are assessing. Nothing here claims NHS approval or universal compliance.

Data protection

Clarify controller and processor roles, data flows, lawful basis, retention, hosting and any international transfers.

Clinical safety

Assess whether DCB0129 and DCB0160 apply. Request release-specific safety documentation, residual risks and local deployment responsibilities.
